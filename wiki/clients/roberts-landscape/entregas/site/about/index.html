Who We Are

A Stone Mason, Not a Sales Office

Polaris Masonry & Hardscape was built on a simple conviction: masonry is permanent, so it should be done by people who treat it that way. We are craftsmen first. The estimate is honest, the base is right, and the stone is set by hand.

When you hire us, you work directly with the person responsible for the work, from the first walk-through to the last stone. No subcontractor maze, no runaround, no surprises on the final invoice.

We serve premium homeowners across Cape Cod and the South Shore who would rather pay once for something that lasts than twice for something that does not.
